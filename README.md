# WorldGeo-AdventureAddon

WorldGeo-AdventureAddon is a wilderness-first regional economy and adventure addon for Minecraft servers built on top of **IMYVMWorldGeo**.

Instead of requiring large prebuilt cities, it turns named regions, scopes, anchors, and travel lines into playable loops:

1. certify local goods with regional identity
2. fulfill delivery and exchange loops
3. store goods as warehouse receipts
4. follow market demand and rumor shifts
5. fund public projects through share subscription
6. allow limited, risk-capped share transfers
7. expand toward frontier escort and relic routes

## 0.1.x focus

The first playable slice is intentionally narrow:

1. WorldGeo region and scope integration
2. right-click anchor interaction
3. local delivery contracts
4. basic exchange flow
5. warehouse and market board
6. public project subscription
7. one frontier escort line

## Design boundaries

The addon is built around a few hard constraints:

1. wilderness and wasteland maps come first
2. minimal anchors are valid content
3. controlled finance must stay tied to real throughput
4. speculation stays capped and secondary
5. regional identity matters more than raw vanilla stockpiles

## Planned systems

As implementation fills in, the project is intended to support:

1. region-certified goods with origin lore
2. layered currencies for livelihood, commerce, and expeditions
3. warehouse receipts and quote-driven market boards
4. public infrastructure projects with real service revenue
5. primary share subscription plus a limited secondary market
6. rumor-driven demand changes and rotating hotspots
7. frontier logistics with failure risk and controlled insurance
